## Safety and official-release readiness

This source tree is not legal advice, a filing-ready legal service, or proof of legal accuracy. Software test results do not establish that generated legal analysis is correct. Outputs require source verification and qualified human review before reliance; the project's attorney-review, privacy, and fail-closed filing safeguards remain in place.

For releases that the maintainers designate as production or Enterprise GA, the project's qualification process continues to require official-source ingestion, attorney-reviewed evaluation, verified release metrics, security and controlled-pilot evidence, and maintainer signoffs. These are official-project release criteria and safety recommendations, not additional restrictions on downstream rights under Apache-2.0.

The software remains provided without warranty under LICENSE.md. See [Safety](docs/safety.md), [Privacy](docs/privacy.md), and [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for operational guidance.
