# Safety Policy

This project provides legal information, not legal advice. It does not create an attorney-client relationship and does not produce filing-ready documents.

The answer path refuses substantive legal, procedure, or form claims when no supporting source is retrieved. It includes citations and effective-date/version warnings when source metadata requires caution.

Domestic violence, protection-from-abuse, child-safety, and immediate-danger prompts route to safety language and official or emergency resources. They are not handled as normal drafting tasks.

No tests or fixtures should include private client facts.
