# Source Policy

Maine Family Law LLM is official-source-first. Legal, procedure, form, and child-support answers must be grounded in retrieved source snippets from the source manifest.

The seed manifest is representative, not complete. A source entry must preserve title, source type, jurisdiction, official flag, URL, effective date, retrieval timestamp, version label, citation hint, terms note, priority, and notes.

Secondary sources may help users orient themselves, but they cannot be treated as official authority and cannot outrank official Maine sources.

Live fetches are optional and limited to public official Maine source URLs. Offline tests use small safe fixtures under `data/fixtures`.
