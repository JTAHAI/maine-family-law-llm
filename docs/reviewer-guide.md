# Reviewer guide

This repository is ready for structured review preparation, not legal GA.

Reviewers can help by checking whether outputs are understandable, source status is visible, and legal claims remain review-required unless verified. Licensed Maine attorney review is different from usability feedback: only real attorney review artifacts can support future attorney-review evidence.

Useful reviewer questions:

- Are the Maine sources real, current, and correctly described?
- Are citations and quote spans verified or clearly marked unresolved?
- Are missing facts, jurisdiction issues, safety risks, and confidentiality risks visible?
- If a protection from abuse issue overlaps with a family case, does the output
  flag the independent-analysis concern described in
  [Protection From Abuse and Family Case Overlap](pfa-family-case-overlap.md)?
- Does the output avoid legal advice, guaranteed outcomes, and filing-ready claims?
- Is the plain-language explanation understandable without hiding uncertainty?

What does not count as review evidence:

- This guide.
- Email templates.
- Sample evidence.
- Unsent outreach materials.
- Informal plans without real correspondence or signed review artifacts.
