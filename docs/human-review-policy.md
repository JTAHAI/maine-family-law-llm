# Human review policy

Human review is required by default for legal, drafting, citation, quote, evidence, and filing-readiness outputs.

Reviewer feedback can improve usability and safety, but not all feedback counts the same way. Licensed Maine attorney review is required before feedback can support attorney-review evidence unless a supervised law-school review explicitly documents attorney involvement.

UMF and UNE usability or pre-law feedback can help improve clarity, but it does not replace licensed Maine attorney review by itself.

Review evidence must be real. Templates, demo journeys, generated sample evidence, and unsent outreach materials do not count.

Reviewer packets created by the repository are deterministic preparation artifacts. They help a reviewer see the prompt, output, status labels, red flags, missing information, citation/quote status, and feedback fields, but they do not prove review happened until a real reviewer supplies evidence.
