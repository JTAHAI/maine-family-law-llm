How to Add Your Corpus

Use the launcher if you want the easiest path. Click Create New Case Corpus for a first build, or Reopen Intake / Add More Evidence when you want to add more material later.

What you can add

  • Downloaded court PDFs, notices, motions, orders, and exhibits
  • Email export folders or individual .eml files from Gmail, Outlook, Hotmail, or Workspace
  • Phone screenshot folders, camera-roll exports, scanned batches, and USB copies
  • School, counseling, therapy, medical, and provider records
  • Word files, spreadsheets, text files, and mixed evidence folders

Good first places to look

  • Downloads: court filings, PDF notices, email attachments, portal exports
  • Documents: prepared filings, letters, school records, case folders
  • Desktop: recent exports or temporary review folders
  • Pictures: screenshots, scans, and phone image exports

Email and phone guidance

If your evidence still lives in webmail or on a phone, export or copy it to this computer first. The app then ingests those folders read-only and keeps them attached to the case for future rebuilds.

When you reopen intake later, you only need to add the new material. Previously remembered source folders stay included automatically unless the underlying drive or folder is missing.

The tool hashes sources before processing and never mutates the originals.
