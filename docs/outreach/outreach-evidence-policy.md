# Outreach evidence policy

Outreach materials do not count as outreach evidence.

Valid evidence can include real correspondence, a scheduled review confirmation, signed reviewer feedback, or a redacted review artifact that records who reviewed what and under what role. Evidence must be accurate, consent-aware, and safe to store.

Invalid evidence includes templates, contact schemas, unsent drafts, sample rows, plans, screenshots of unsent messages, or statements that someone agreed to review when they did not.

Attorney review evidence requires a licensed Maine attorney or an explicitly supervised review path with documented attorney involvement. UMF and UNE usability feedback may be useful, but it does not replace licensed attorney review by itself.
