    <div class="section-intro">
      <div class="eyebrow">Three separate lanes</div>
      <h2>Authority, private evidence, and model analysis never become the same thing.</h2>
    </div>
    <div class="grid-3">
      <article class="card">
        <div class="card-number">A</div>
        <h3>Official Maine authority</h3>
        <p>Admitted external sources carry official URLs, hashes, retrieval dates, freshness, lineage, citations, and exact spans.</p>
      </article>
      <article class="card">
        <div class="card-number">B</div>
        <h3>Private matter records</h3>
        <p>User-selected records stay scoped to the active matter. They may support facts, but they are never treated as legal authority.</p>
      </article>
      <article class="card">
        <div class="card-number">C</div>
        <h3>Model analysis</h3>
        <p>Generated explanations and drafts remain review-required and cannot silently outrank official sources or the record.</p>
      </article>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

      <div class="eyebrow">Verified v7 workflow</div>
      <h2>From selected records to a reviewable work product.</h2>
    </div>
    <div class="grid-3">
      <article class="card">
        <h3>Import and inspect</h3>
        <p>Inventory mixed records, preserve originals, create searchable OCR derivatives, scan privacy, and compare duplicate or changed copies.</p>
      </article>
      <article class="card">
        <h3>Research and verify</h3>
        <p>Ask Maine-law questions, open official source cards, inspect exact passages, and verify citations, pinpoints, quotes, and claim support.</p>
      </article>
      <article class="card">
        <h3>Draft and review</h3>
        <p>Create review-required drafts, retain immutable revisions, compare changes, and generate blocker-bearing packets and receipts.</p>
      </article>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>
