# Maine Family Law LLM

Maine Family Law LLM is an open-source, local-first legal-information and record-organization workbench for Maine family-law matters. It helps users organize documents, build timelines and evidence maps, review citations, identify missing information, and create review-required working drafts. It is not a lawyer, does not provide legal advice, and is not affiliated with the Maine Judicial Branch or any court.

## Why it exists

Families often face fragmented records, difficult evidence organization, information asymmetry, and practical barriers to reviewing what is already in the record. Maine Family Law LLM is designed to help users navigate those materials more carefully and transparently.

## What it does

- organizes selected records into a local review workspace
- builds timelines, issue lanes, and evidence maps
- returns source-grounded answers or says not found in indexed corpus
- supports review-required drafts and checklists
- keeps original source files read-only
- exports reviewer packages with verification metadata

## Important boundaries

- not legal advice
- not a replacement for a lawyer
- review required
- not affiliated with the Maine Judicial Branch, any court, or any government body
- default operation is local-first
- private matter data is not used for shared-model training by default

## Build one for your state

This Maine edition is intentionally open source. Parents, advocates, legal-aid organizations, attorneys, researchers, and technologists in other states are invited to fork the public repository and build verified state-specific editions.

A safe state edition requires that state's official statutes, rules, forms, case law, citation standards, freshness controls, evaluation data, and qualified human review. Maine authority must not be reused as authority for another jurisdiction.
