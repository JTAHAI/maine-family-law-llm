Appeals

The Maine Judicial Branch appeals page explains that most District Court appeals are filed directly to the Supreme Judicial Court. Small claims appeals and eviction appeals are filed in the Superior Court.

Appeals from the Superior Court may be taken to the Supreme Judicial Court. The Supreme Judicial Court is the State's highest court and the court of final appeal. It is often called the Law Court when deciding appeals.

The Court's major job is to decide appeals on questions of law. Parties or lawyers present written briefs and oral arguments. Appeal questions require checking the current Maine Rules of Appellate Procedure, deadlines, record requirements, and any family-matter-specific posture.
