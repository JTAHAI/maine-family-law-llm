Maine Court Forms

Family forms

Family matter packets and child support forms are listed by the Maine Judicial Branch. Verify the current form number and instructions before use.

Example form reference: FM-050 Child Support Affidavit appears on public family form listings.

Official family forms may include parentage forms, child support forms, fee waiver or filing fee forms, proof of service paperwork, and packets that vary by case type. Always verify the current form version, required fields, attachments, and instructions before filing.

Saved packets can become stale. Form selection should be checked against the current official forms page, especially for parentage, divorce, parental rights, child support, protection from abuse, service, and fee waiver issues.
