Help and tips

How this workbench is meant to be used

Ask a grounded question, inspect the answer, then drill into the source cards and reviewer handoff before relying on it. This tool is local-only and not legal advice.

Chat-first flow Use the main chat for the question, then open the Evidence & tools drawer for source cards, role tuning, corpus switching, and reviewer handoff.
  • Copy settings link preserves only role, style, topic, and search mode. It excludes questions, context, records, corpus paths, and local paths.
  • Evidence & tools opens sources, matter controls, review details, and starter prompts only when you need them.
  • New chat clears the transcript but keeps the active corpus and role.
Evidence review Source cards should be the first stop after an answer. Use them to audit what the workbench actually relied on.
  • Inspect source opens the local metadata payload for that card.
  • Copy source cards exports the current card set for a reviewer or file memo.
  • Load source list lets you inspect available sources even before asking.
Reviewer handoff The handoff panel surfaces missing information, follow-up questions, and matched library metadata for human review.
  • Use the Missing information or Source-card table answer styles for denser audit trails.
  • Use Save transcript or Download JSON for a portable local record.
Adding family records Use the desktop launcher's intake wizard to select files, folders, or ZIP exports. It works with PDFs, emails, phone exports, office documents, and common screenshots without modifying the originals.
  • FOCAF family resources and download library are optional external resources, not legal authority.
  • Opening either link is a separate browser action. The workbench does not send your matter name, records, question, or search terms.

Privacy by design

Your family’s matter stays local

This workbench is designed to process the chat and private matter records on this device. It does not silently send your question, matter context, or local corpus path to a remote model service.

Stored locallyInstalled matter indexes, app state, exports you create, and local diagnostics remain on this device unless you deliberately move or share them.
External links are separateOpening an official source in your browser may connect to that website. The workbench marks that as a separate action.
Sharing is explicitCopy, transcript, JSON, and reviewer-handoff actions only run after you choose them.
Review before sendingExports can contain sensitive family information. Read them before emailing, uploading, filing, or giving them to another person.
Local-first does not mean risk-free. Protect exported files like any other sensitive family record.
